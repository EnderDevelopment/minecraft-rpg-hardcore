# DUSKRYN: Realm Of The Forsworn

DUSKRYN: Realm Of The Forsworn is a challenging Minecraft modpack designed for Minecraft 1.20.x, focusing on hardcore RPG progression, difficult survival, bosses, dungeons, magic, rare equipment, world exploration, player-versus-player combat, multiplayer compatibility, and mandatory shader support. This modpack offers a deep and engaging RPG experience with a high difficulty level, ensuring that players must prepare thoroughly for the challenges that lie ahead.

## Install

1. Download and install the Minecraft Forge or NeoForge mod loader for Minecraft 1.20.x.
2. Create a new Minecraft Forge or NeoForge profile in your launcher.
3. Download the DUSKRYN: Realm Of The Forsworn modpack from the GitHub repository or the EnderDevelopment marketplace.
4. Extract the contents of the downloaded modpack into the mods folder of your Minecraft Forge or NeoForge profile.
5. Launch Minecraft using the Forge or NeoForge profile.

## Use

1. Start a new world or load an existing one.
2. Explore the world and encounter various challenges, including dangerous mobs, elite enemies, mini-bosses, and powerful bosses.
3. Progress through the game by earning experience points, leveling up, and unlocking new abilities and equipment.
4. Engage in player-versus-player combat to test your skills against other players.
5. Utilize magic and rare equipment to overcome the toughest challenges and progress further in the game.
6. Enjoy the immersive world and the deep RPG progression system that DUSKRYN: Realm Of The Forsworn offers.
